Our Heritage & Legacy

A Symphony of Flavors, Spices & Royal Hospitality

Welcome to Restaurant, where ancient Indian culinary heritage meets contemporary fine dining. Our journey began with a passion for preserving ancestral recipes passed down through royal kitchens across the sub-continent.

Every dish is a tribute to India’s diverse food culture—from the fragrant biryanis of Hyderabad to the charcoal-roasted kebabs of Lucknow and coastal delicacies of the South. We source organic spices directly from artisanal farms to guarantee purity and unmatched depth of flavor.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How far in advance should I make a table reservation?

We recommend booking 2 to 3 days in advance for weekend dinners or special occasions. For weekday lunches, same-day bookings are usually accommodated based on availability.

2. Do you offer complimentary valet parking?

Yes, we provide 100% complimentary secure valet parking for all our dining guests upon arrival at our main entrance.

3. Are there Jain and pure vegetarian options available?

Absolutely. We maintain a separate dedicated vegetarian kitchen line and offer an extensive range of Jain options cooked without onion or garlic upon request.

4. Do you cater for private parties and corporate events?

Yes, our Royal Banquet Hall and Private VIP Chambers can accommodate gatherings from 15 to 200 guests with custom menu selection and dedicated event staff.

5. What is the dress code at Restaurant?

We maintain a Smart Casual to Formal dress code. We kindly request guests to avoid athletic wear, beachwear, or flip-flops during evening dining hours.

6. Do you offer home delivery and gourmet takeaway?

Yes! We offer tamper-proof, temperature-controlled eco-friendly packaging for takeaway orders and direct luxury home delivery within a 10 km radius.

7. Can I customize spice levels in my dishes?

Yes, every dish is cooked fresh to order. You can specify Mild, Medium, Authentic Indian, or Extra Spicy when placing your order with our service captain.

8. Are children welcome at the restaurant?

We are a family-friendly establishment! We offer high chairs, a specially curated non-spicy Kids Menu, and kid-friendly dessert platters.

9. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major International Credit Cards, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, UPI payments, Apple Pay, and Cash.

10. Is corkage allowed for personal celebration wines?

We permit personal vintage wine bottles for private celebratory dinners with a nominal corkage fee. Please notify our reservations team in advance.
